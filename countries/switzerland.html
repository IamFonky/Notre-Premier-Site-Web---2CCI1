The big picture

Where the mountains meet the sky

Switzerland is a landlocked country in the heart of Europe. The Alps cover much of its landscape, with snowy peaks, green valleys, and sparkling lakes. People come from all over the world to hike, ski, and enjoy the scenery. Switzerland is also famous for chocolate, cheese, and clever inventions like the Swiss Army knife.

Did you know?
