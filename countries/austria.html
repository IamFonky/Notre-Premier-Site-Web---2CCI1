Discover Austria

A country of alpine landscapes, historic cities, and musical traditions.

Mountains and nature

The Austrian Alps cover much of the country. Visitors explore mountain trails, lakes, and villages throughout the year.

Capital city: Vienna

Vienna is known for its grand architecture, classical music, cafés, and museums. It is Austria’s largest city.

Quick facts

  • Capital: Vienna
  • Language: German
  • Currency: Euro
